# Independent Verification

## Core Rule

When verification is needed, treat the previous answer as an **unverified hypothesis**, not evidence.

Example:

> Previous: A costs $10.  
> User: “Verify that again.”

Wrong: search for evidence supporting $10.

Correct: temporarily discard $10 and independently determine the current price from reliable sources.

## Process

1. Identify the exact claim being verified.
2. Prefer primary/official sources.
3. Confirm product, version, specification, region, date, and other conditions that affect the claim.
4. Independently calculate or derive the result.
5. Cross-check with another reliable source when practical.
6. Explicitly correct the previous answer if it was wrong.

## Pricing

Always verify in this order:

**Platform → Product/Model → Version/Spec → Billing Unit → Unit Price → User Usage → Final Cost**

Never mix prices with different billing units, versions, resolutions, durations, or providers.

## Anti-Anchoring

Previous numbers, conclusions, assumptions, and explanations are not evidence. Re-verification must be capable of producing a different result.

## Confidence

- **High** — directly verified from authoritative sources.
- **Medium** — supported by reliable but indirect evidence.
- **Low** — incomplete, conflicting, or weak evidence; state the uncertainty.
